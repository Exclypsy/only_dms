# Privacy Policy – NoFeed

_Last updated: 30 September 2026_

NoFeed is a small app that shows the Instagram website (instagram.com)
limited to direct messages, your profile and the posts of people you follow. It
is an independent project, not affiliated with
Meta Platforms, Inc. or Instagram.

## What the app collects

**Nothing leaves your device.** The app has no server, no analytics, no
advertising SDKs and no crash reporting. The developer never receives any of
your data.

- You log in directly on instagram.com inside the app. The app does not read,
  store, log or send your password, cookies, tokens or browsing history.
- The JavaScript the app adds to the page inserts a style sheet that hides some
  buttons. It reads only these things, all of which stay on your device:
  - your own username and the address of your own profile picture, so the
    Profile button can open your profile and show your picture (the picture is
    loaded from Instagram's image server and kept only in memory);
  - if notifications are on: the name and the preview text of unread chats in
    the chat list (see Notifications);
  - for instant chats: whether an opened chat has finished loading and is
    scrolled to its newest message – a number, no text.
- Messages of an opened conversation are never read as text. With instant chats
  on, a picture of the chat is kept on the device (see below).

## Permissions

- **Internet** – to load instagram.com.
- **Camera / microphone** – asked only when instagram.com requests them (e.g.
  voice messages, taking a photo for a message) and only used by the
  Instagram page. On iPhone, iOS asks you before first use.
- **Photos** – you pick them in the system Photo Picker; the app gets access
  only to the items you choose and does not request storage access.

## Notifications (optional, off by default)

If you turn on notifications in Settings, NoFeed reads the name and the preview
text of unread chats from Instagram's chat list and shows them in a local
notification, like the Instagram app does. They are kept only in memory while
the app runs, are not written to disk and are not sent anywhere. Whether the
text is visible on the lock screen is controlled by your phone's notification
settings. On Android, NoFeed stays running in the background (with a permanent
notification) while this is on.

## Instant chats (on by default, can be turned off)

So that a chat opens at once, NoFeed keeps a picture (screenshot) of each chat
you have opened – at most 30 chats – in the app's private cache folder. The
pictures never leave the device, are not included in backups, and on iPhone
are encrypted while the phone is locked. They are deleted when you log out or
turn off Settings → Súkromie → "Okamžité otváranie chatov". NoFeed never opens
chats on its own.

## What is stored on your device

Like any web browser, the built-in WebView stores cookies, cache and local
storage for instagram.com so you stay logged in. This data:

- stays only on your device,
- together with your app settings, your Instagram username for the Profile
  button (read from the inbox header; deleted when you log out) and, if instant
  chats are on, the pictures of opened chats described above,
- is excluded from Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer.

## How to delete it

- Uninstall the app, or clear its storage in Android Settings → Apps → NoFeed
  → Storage → Clear storage.
- Settings (long-press Profile) → **Log out and delete data** (also deletes the
  chat pictures).
- On iPhone: delete the app, which removes all its data.

Data you send to Instagram (messages, photos) is processed by Meta under
[Instagram's privacy policy](https://privacycenter.instagram.com/policy).
